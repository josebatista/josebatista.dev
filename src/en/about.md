I'm **José Batista de Andrade Pereira**, a Senior/Staff+ Software Engineer with over a decade of experience building mobile, embedded, desktop, and web software. I have a strong foundation in Android and mobile SDKs and currently work with platform observability and real-time data processing. My experience spans digital fraud prevention, payments, education, and IoT.

**Currently**, I am a Staff Software Engineer at PicPay, contributing to the evolution of the observability platform and its Android SDK with a focus on performance, reliability, and scale. I work on real-time telemetry processing with OpenTelemetry, Kafka, and Apache Flink, as well as the microservices that support these data flows. My responsibilities include automated incident analysis with Python, BigQuery, and Crashlytics; troubleshooting distributed systems with Grafana, Databricks, and OpenSearch; and metric-cardinality studies that balance granularity, cost, and performance while improving the quality and usefulness of metrics and dashboards.

I specialize in native Android architecture with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, MVVM, Hilt, and Gradle Kotlin DSL; security-focused mobile SDKs; and scalable Gradle modularization through convention plugins. I also build delivery pipelines with GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Bitbucket Pipelines, Azure Pipelines, Bitrise, and fastlane. My experience includes on-device AI for document detection and facial liveness, shared libraries with Kotlin Multiplatform, and support for React Native and Flutter projects.

Throughout my career, I have built security-focused mobile solutions for fraud prevention, including real-time document detection and facial-liveness integrations. I have also worked on protecting payment data in transit and on TEF and POS solutions, contributing to payment-infrastructure solutions and terminal applications used throughout Brazil. Across roles in payments and personal finance, I have led architecture, improved build engineering, and mentored engineers. I value clear technical decisions, maintainable systems, and sharing knowledge within teams.

**Education:** I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and an MBA in Software Engineering. I am currently pursuing an MBA in Applied AI Software Engineering.

**Core technologies:** Kotlin, Java, Go, Python, C/C++, JavaScript, OpenTelemetry, Apache Kafka, and Apache Flink.
