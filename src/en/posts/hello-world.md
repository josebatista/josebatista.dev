## Hello, world!

Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I'm José Batista, a Senior/Staff+ Software Engineer with a background in Android, mobile SDKs, payments, and digital security. Today, my work also goes deep into platform observability and real-time telemetry processing: the systems, data flows, and decisions that make software easier to understand, operate, and improve.

This blog is where I want to turn that day-to-day experience into useful, well-reasoned writing. It will be a place to document ideas, technical decisions, investigations, and lessons that are worth revisiting.

## What to expect

The posts here will mostly explore:

- **Observability at scale:** OpenTelemetry, Kafka, Apache Flink, telemetry pipelines, metric cardinality, dashboards, and incident investigation.
- **Android and mobile SDK engineering:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Gradle modularization, build tooling, performance, and reliable developer-facing APIs.
- **Security and payments:** fraud prevention, on-device document detection, biometric flows, and data protection.
- **Software engineering practice:** architecture, delivery pipelines, technical leadership, and the trade-offs behind maintainable systems.

Some posts will be deep technical explorations. Others may be shorter notes about a problem, a decision, or an approach that proved useful. The common thread will be practical engineering: understanding the context, making deliberate choices, and learning from the result.

## Why write?

Writing is one of the best ways I know to make an idea clearer. It creates room to look beyond the immediate solution, explain the reasoning behind it, and leave a trail that may help someone else facing a similar problem.

## How I use AI on this blog

All content on this blog will be written by me, based on my experience and studies. I will use AI as an editorial tool to make the writing clearer and more accessible and to support the translation of content into English. All texts will be reviewed before publication to avoid inaccuracies or gaps that could undermine learning. AI is a powerful tool, not a crutch to replace my own work or learning.

This is only the beginning. Thanks for being here.
